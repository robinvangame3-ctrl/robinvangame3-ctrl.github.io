Privacy Policy — Merry Mint

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Merry Mint is developed and operated by Sharvesh Digumber (“we”, “us” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Merry Mint, use its optional online rankings or contact us.

Privacy contact: robinvangame3@gmail.com

1. Information stored on your device

Merry Mint stores information locally to provide gameplay and remember your progress. This may include:

  • Your current or paused game.
  • Scores, records and recent game history.
  • Daily challenge results and medals.
  • Winter Trail progress and unlocked cosmetics.
  • Your Snow coin balance, rewards, purchases and transaction history.
  • Sound, music, vibration, appearance and control preferences.
  • Your online profile details and private access credential, if you join rankings.
  • Completed game inputs awaiting online score verification.

The Android app uses local application storage. The browser version uses browser storage.

Clearing application or browser storage may permanently remove local information. Android players can choose Account & cloud saves to sign in with Google and enable backups.

Google Firebase Authentication verifies the sign-in. We use the linked account identifier and email address to connect you to your account. If you enable backup, our cloud service stores a copy of your game progress, records, settings and wallet data so you can restore it while signed in.

2. Optional online rankings

You can play the offline modes without creating an online profile. Ranked Sprint requires an online profile and an internet connection. Browsing online leaderboards also connects to our ranking service.

When you join rankings, we create and store:

  • A randomly generated player identifier.
  • A generated nickname.
  • Your optional chosen public name or nickname.
  • The country you choose to represent.
  • Profile creation information.
  • A protected hash of your private access credential.

You do not need to provide an email address, telephone number or real name to join.

Your country is selected by you. Merry Mint does not use GPS to determine or verify your country.

3. Public leaderboard information

If you submit a score that passes verification, other people may see:

  • Your chosen public name, or your generated nickname.
  • Your selected country.
  • Your verified score and leaderboard position.
  • Associated game information, such as your highest coin and the relevant leaderboard period.

Leaderboard information may be viewed by people who have not joined rankings.

Your private access credential and internal player identifier are not displayed on public leaderboards.

You can change your public name in Rankings → My online profile. Leaving the name blank restores your original generated nickname. Your updated name appears beside your existing online scores.

Names are not verified identities and may be used by more than one player. We recommend using a nickname if you do not want your real name to be public. Other people may copy or take screenshots of publicly displayed information.

4. Game inputs and score verification

To check that submitted scores follow the game rules, Merry Mint sends information about the completed round to our server. This includes:

  • Your player and challenge identifiers.
  • Drop positions and their timing.
  • The claimed score and round duration.
  • Information identifying the relevant game rules.

The server replays the round before accepting the score. Verification may use temporary checkpoints containing game state and processed input history.

Daily score submission is initiated by the player. Eligible connected Classic and Ranked results are submitted automatically when the round finishes.

Pending submissions may remain stored locally and resume when you reopen the app or reconnect.

Completed leaderboard records retain score summaries and verification metadata, rather than the complete raw drop history.

5. Technical information

Connecting to online features sends normal network information to our hosting provider. This may include your IP address, request timestamps, request details and technical error or security logs.

We use online identifiers, challenge records, verification information and request counters to:

  • Authenticate access to your profile.
  • Operate rankings and country tables.
  • Verify scores and prevent duplicate submissions.
  • Limit excessive requests and protect the service.
  • Investigate technical problems and suspected abuse.

Offline gameplay does not require a connection to our ranking service.

6. How we use information

We process information to:

  • Provide gameplay and save local progress.
  • Remember your settings and cosmetic choices.
  • Maintain your optional online profile.
  • Display verified scores and rankings.
  • Verify completed rounds.
  • Protect the game and ranking service.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We use leaderboard names and gameplay inputs for gameplay, score verification and rankings. Ad providers process the data described in section 7.

7. Advertising and purchases

Merry Mint uses Google AdMob for banners and full-screen ads after completed rounds. Players can choose to watch a rewarded video for Snow coins. Premium removes banners and automatic full-screen ads.

Google and the advertising partners listed in the consent message may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising or other app and device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics. These data help deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and personalise ads where permitted.

Where required, Google’s consent form lets you choose how your data is used. In the app, open Settings and choose Ad privacy choices to revisit those choices where available. Your choices and ad availability determine whether you see personalised, non-personalised or limited ads.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Snow coins are fictional game currency. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money.

Google Play Billing processes optional purchases and subscriptions. We use purchase information, such as the purchased item and purchase token, to verify transactions and restore eligible purchases. Google Play handles payment details and subscription settings.

10. Retention

Local information: Local saves, settings, currency and pending submissions remain on your device until removed through the app, operating system or browser, or replaced during normal use. Uninstallation may remove local information, depending on your device and backup settings.

Online profiles and scores: We retain your online profile and verified score records to operate rankings unless you delete your profile or we remove information for maintenance, security or legal reasons. The current service does not automatically delete every inactive profile or historical score after a fixed period.

Verification information: Challenges expire after 24 hours, but expiry does not necessarily delete the corresponding database record. Temporary verification checkpoints are cleared when verification is accepted or rejected, or when the associated profile is deleted. Unfinished or expired records may remain until cleanup or profile deletion.

Logs and backups: Hosting logs and any provider-maintained backups may follow separate retention arrangements and may not disappear immediately when an active database record is deleted.

We may retain information longer where required by law or necessary to resolve disputes or investigate abuse.

11. Your choices and deletion

Request deletion of your Merry Mint account

Email an account deletion request. Include the email address used to sign in and, if relevant, your leaderboard nickname and country. We may ask for information to verify your request. Keep your password and private game credential confidential.

For verified requests, we delete the Merry Mint cloud account, its backup and associated records. If you also use online rankings, tell us so we can locate the ranking profile and its scores. We may retain records as needed for fraud prevention, security, disputes or legal obligations. Manage any active subscription in Google Play.

You can:

  • Use offline modes without joining online rankings.
  • Choose a nickname instead of your real name.
  • Change your public name or restore your generated nickname.
  • Disable optional music, sound effects or combo vibrations.
  • Delete local information through your device or browser settings.
  • Delete your online profile through the app.

To delete your online profile, open:

Rankings → My online profile → Delete online profile & scores

This removes your active online profile, associated leaderboard scores, challenges and verification checkpoints. Your local gameplay records and currency remain on your device unless you remove them separately.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app does not, by itself, send an online profile deletion request. Delete your online profile first if you also want it removed from the ranking service.

Your private access credential is stored locally. Clearing storage may remove your ability to access or delete the profile through the app. If you enabled a cloud backup before clearing storage, sign in with the same Google account and restore that backup.

If you need help, contact robinvangame3@gmail.com. We may need sufficient information to identify the correct profile and verify that a request is authorised. Please do not send your private access credential by email.

12. Security

We use measures designed to protect information, including encrypted HTTPS connections, private database access controls and authentication for profile changes, score submissions and deletion.

The ranking database stores a hash of your private access credential rather than the credential itself.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect access to your device and do not share private credentials or unredacted application save files.

  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Consent where required for a particular feature or activity.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority. In Malta, this is the Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner:

https://idpc.org.mt/

To make a privacy request, contact robinvangame3@gmail.com. Identity verification may be necessary, particularly because online profiles do not require email registration.

14. Children’s privacy

Merry Mint does not require players to provide their real name or contact details.

Children should use a nickname and avoid putting personal information in a public leaderboard name. A parent or guardian should assist where necessary.

Where applicable law requires parental authorisation for a child’s use of online features, that authorisation must be obtained before joining rankings.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact robinvangame3@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Merry Mint does not automatically post your results to social networks or contact other people on your behalf.
